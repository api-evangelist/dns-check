DNS Check can now send a signed HTTPS request to any URL you choose whenever a monitored DNS record starts failing or starts passing again. If you've ever copied the details out of a DNS Check alert email into a ticket or a script, a webhook can do that step for you. Why Webhooks DNS Check already tells people when a DNS record changes via email, Slack, PagerDuty, Opsgenie, and other integrations .
